Privacy policy · Steps 1.1.1 · 10 November 2026

Steps is published by Tamarin. Contact: tamarin.dev@proton.me.

Local journal data

Your goals, answers, actions and reflections are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData. This release has no account, no ads inside the app and no developer-operated journal server. The developer does not receive your journal content.

Measuring our ads

Steps includes Meta's SDK (FacebookCore) so we can tell whether our ads on Facebook and Instagram lead people to install the app. When Steps is installed and opened, the SDK sends Meta app events about the install and the launch, with technical details such as the device model, the iOS version, the app version, the language and the time zone, and an identifier the SDK creates for this installation. Meta measures the results of our ads through Apple's SKAdNetwork. Steps never asks for permission to track you and does not share your device's advertising identifier, and your goals, answers, actions and reflections are never sent to Meta. Meta handles this data under its own privacy policy.

Our download page

Our ads link to a download page at stepsjournal.github.io, which counts its visits and the taps on its download buttons with Umami, an analytics service that does not use cookies. It records the page address, including the label of the ad that brought you there, which button you tapped, whether the page stayed open for at least ten seconds, the site you came from and general details such as your browser, device type, language and country. It does not identify you, and nothing from the app or your journal is involved. Umami handles this data under its own privacy policy.

Reminders

With your permission, iOS schedules a local daily notification. It does not contain your journal text. You can change its time or disable it in the app or iOS Settings.

Backups and exports

Your device backup may include app data according to your Apple backup settings. An export is created only when you choose to export and save or share it. You control its destination and any subsequent copies.

Migration

When upgrading from numeric ratings, the original journal document is kept locally as a recovery copy. Actions remain available, and old ratings are not treated as Yes or Not today. The recovery copy is removed with Delete all journal data.

Retention and deletion

Data remains in the app until you delete it. In Settings, choose Delete all journal data to remove the app's goals, answers, actions and reflections and cancel its reminder. Exported copies and existing device backups must be managed separately. No backend identity is created in this release.

AI and purchases

No AI service, subscription, or other paid feature is active in this version. If such features are introduced, the policy will be updated and explicit permission will be requested before personal text is sent to a third-party AI provider.

Contact and updates

For any privacy question or request, write to tamarin.dev@proton.me. Material changes will be reflected in the app and on this page.
