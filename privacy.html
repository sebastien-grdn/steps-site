Privacy policy · Steps 1.0 free offline edition · 28 October 2026

Steps is published by Tamarin. Contact: tamarin.dev@proton.me.

Local journal data

Your goals, answers, actions and reflections are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData. This release has no account, advertising, analytics or developer-operated journal server. The developer does not receive your journal content.

Reminders

With your permission, iOS schedules a local daily notification. It does not contain your journal text. You can change its time or disable it in the app or iOS Settings.

Backups and exports

Your device backup may include app data according to your Apple backup settings. An export is created only when you choose to export and save or share it. You control its destination and any subsequent copies.

Migration

When upgrading from numeric ratings, the original journal document is kept locally as a recovery copy. Actions remain available, and old ratings are not treated as Yes or Not today. The recovery copy is removed with Delete all journal data.

Retention and deletion

Data remains in the app until you delete it. In Settings, choose Delete all journal data to remove the app's goals, answers, actions and reflections and cancel its reminder. Exported copies and existing device backups must be managed separately. No backend identity is created in this release.

AI and purchases

No AI service, subscription, or other paid feature is active in this version. If such features are introduced, the policy will be updated and explicit permission will be requested before personal text is sent to a third-party AI provider.

Contact and updates

For any privacy question or request, write to tamarin.dev@proton.me. Material changes will be reflected in the app and on this page.
